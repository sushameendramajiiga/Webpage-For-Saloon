Our Story

Where Beauty Meets Expertise

Akshai Unisex Salon was established in Pragathi Nagar, Hyderabad with a vision to deliver premium beauty and grooming services to every guest — men, women, and everyone in between. Our passionate team of skilled professionals uses top brands like Matrix and TRU Hair & Skin to ensure nothing less than excellence.

From transformative hair colour and balayage to relaxing spa treatments, bridal packages, and advanced skin care — Akshai is your one-stop destination for looking and feeling extraordinary.
